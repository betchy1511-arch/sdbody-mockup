Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) delivers oxygen at increased pressure inside a chamber. At Hormones and Wellness by SD Body, HBOT is available at our La Jolla clinic and begins with a clinician evaluation of candidacy, protocol, and monitoring.

Available at our La Jolla clinic

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is offered exclusively at La Jolla, 7710 Fay Ave. A clinician reviews whether it is appropriate for your goals and health before any session is scheduled.

What an evaluation reviews

  • Your goals and current health information
  • Candidacy and any contraindications
  • Protocol and session monitoring
  • Provider oversight throughout

How care works

  1. Intake and goals.
  2. Physician evaluation of candidacy and health information.
  3. Sessions with provider oversight, if appropriate.
  4. Guidance on follow-up.
