Red light therapy (photobiomodulation) uses specific wavelengths of light. People use it as part of a recovery and wellness routine. At SD Body it is offered alongside physician-led care, not as a stand-alone claim.

What people use it for

  • Recovery and wellness routines
  • Skin and general wellness goals
  • A complement to a broader plan

Where it's available

Red light therapy is available at both our Mission Hills and La Jolla clinics.

Physician-informed, not a stand-alone promise

This practice is run by real medical doctors. Wellness and recovery services are offered alongside a physician-led evaluation, so they fit into a plan rather than replace one. See recovery and longevity care.

Duration depends on the reason for treatment, your response, risks, preferences, and medical guidance. A clinician explains the monitoring plan and how treatment may be changed or stopped.
