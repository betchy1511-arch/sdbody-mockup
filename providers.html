Dr. Shawn Vedamani, MD

Co-Founder & Medical Director

Board-certified anesthesiologist and co-founder of Hormones and Wellness by SD Body, Dr. Vedamani combines over 15 years of clinical experience with a personalized, evidence-based approach to optimizing health from the inside out. His philosophy centers on empowering patients through science-driven care that enhances energy, balance, and long-term vitality.

View provider profile

Dr. Alex Roher, MD

Co-Founder

Dr. Roher is a practicing board-certified anesthesiologist whose real passion is helping people change their lives for the better. In 2012 he founded a San Diego aesthetics and wellness practice built around minimally invasive cosmetic procedures and micro-treatments. Today he is redefining the wellness space with Hormones and Wellness by SD Body.

View provider profile

Brooke Stearns, PA-C

Lead Medical Provider

With nearly a decade of clinical experience, Brooke Stearns, PA-C specializes in hormone optimization, weight management, and longevity medicine for both men and women. She’s passionate about helping patients feel energized, balanced, and confident at every stage of life through personalized, results-driven care.
