The practice

A physician-led practice

Hormones and Wellness by SD Body is a physician-led practice in San Diego, with clinics in Mission Hills and La Jolla. Our clinicians set the clinical standards, protocols, and oversight for every plan — while your consultations and day-to-day care are provided by an experienced team of nurse practitioners and physician assistants.

Why physician-led matters

Most clinics in the hormone and wellness category are not led by physicians. We are. Our physicians define the protocols, testing standards, and clinical oversight behind every plan — not a sales script. Your consultations are provided primarily by our nurse practitioners and physician assistants, working within that physician-led framework. It is the reason patients trust us with the questions that matter.

Two San Diego clinics

We see patients in person at Mission Hills and La Jolla. Each clinic has its own team and hours. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is available at La Jolla.

How care works

Your care begins with a comprehensive evaluation and lab work, followed by a one-on-one consultation and a personalized treatment plan with ongoing monitoring. Explore our process or meet the clinicians behind your care.

Our standard of care

A result is one part of the picture. We read it in context, we explain options and risks honestly, and we only recommend care when it is appropriate. That is the standard at both clinics.
