About

A physician devoted to how you feel, not just your labs

— Shawn Vedamani, MD

Dr. Shawn Vedamani earned his medical degree from the Loma Linda University School of Medicine in Loma Linda, California. After completing his residency, he practiced in private clinics across the United States — including Los Angeles and Oklahoma City — building a broad, patient-first foundation in care.

A Board-Certified Anesthesiologist with over 10 years of experience in the medical field, Dr. Vedamani developed a deep interest in the science of how the body changes over time. That curiosity became a genuine passion for anti-aging and preventative medicine.

“Looking and feeling your best isn’t a luxury — it’s the foundation of a longer, fuller, more active life.”

In 2017, he turned his focus toward regenerative and preventative care, joining a San Diego aesthetics and wellness practice, and in 2018 he founded SD Body. Since then, he has provided safe, evidence-based, customized treatments to every patient who walks through the door.

Dr. Vedamani understands the importance of looking and feeling your best — and he is committed to helping each patient reach those goals with a plan built entirely around them. He sees patients at both the Mission Hills and La Jolla clinics.

Treatment philosophy

Safe, evidence-based, and built around you

Dr. Vedamani believes the best medicine is personal. Every plan starts with listening — to your symptoms, your history, and your goals — then pairs that with objective testing and sound clinical judgment.
