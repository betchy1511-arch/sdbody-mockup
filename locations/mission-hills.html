Questions patients ask before they book

What happens at your first visit in Mission Hills?
The visit begins with your goals, symptoms, and health history. A clinician determines whether an examination or testing is appropriate, then explains the available next steps.
Will I have to stay on hormones forever?
Duration depends on the reason for treatment, response, risks, preferences, and medical guidance. A clinician explains the monitoring plan and how treatment may be changed or stopped.
Is hormone therapy safe? I heard it raises cancer risk.
Benefits and risks vary by patient and treatment. A clinician reviews personal and family history, contraindications, alternatives, and monitoring before recommending care.
Where do I park?
The Mission Hills clinic is on Hancock St with nearby street and lot parking. The front desk can share the most current parking guidance when you book.
Does insurance cover it?
Coverage depends on your plan and your situation. We will give you the honest answer and walk through what is included before you commit.
