About

Optimizing health — not just treating illness

— Brooke Stearns, PA-C

With over 9 years of experience as a Physician Assistant, Brooke began her career in Emergency Medicine, where she developed a strong foundation in acute care and a deep appreciation for the importance of timely, effective treatment. Over time, her passion evolved toward helping people not just avoid illness — but truly optimize their health. This led her to transition out of the hospital setting to focus on helping patients feel and function at their best every day.

At Hormones and Wellness by SD Body, Brooke works with both men and women to support hormone optimization, weight management, and overall longevity. Her goal is to help patients feel energized, balanced, and empowered in their health. She takes a personalized approach to care, offering tailored treatment plans based on each patient’s unique needs — whether that’s fat loss, improved recovery, better sleep, or healthy aging.

Brooke is especially passionate about women’s health, with a strong focus on hormone optimization. She’s a dedicated advocate for access to hormone therapy and supports women through every stage of life — from PMS to perimenopause and postmenopause — with clarity and confidence.

Equally committed to helping men enhance their vitality, energy, and overall well-being, Brooke provides personalized strategies to optimize hormone levels and long-term health. Whether you’re just beginning your wellness journey or refining your current approach, she’s dedicated to helping you achieve lasting, sustainable results. She sees patients at both the Mission Hills and La Jolla clinics.
