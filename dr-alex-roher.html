About

A doctor driven by helping people change their lives

— Alex Roher, MD

Dr. Alex Roher is a practicing, Board-Certified Anesthesiologist whose real passion is helping people change their lives for the better. His training in procedural medicine gave him a meticulous, safety-first approach that carries into everything he does at SD Body.

In 2012, he founded a San Diego aesthetics and wellness practice built around minimally invasive cosmetic procedures and micro-treatments — developing a reputation for natural-looking results and careful, individualized care.

“The goal was never to chase a trend — it was to help people feel like themselves again.”

Today, he is redefining the wellness space as co-founder of SD Body, bringing that same precision and patient-first philosophy to hormone therapy, longevity, and preventative care.

Dr. Roher sees patients at both the Mission Hills and La Jolla clinics, where he partners with each person to build a plan around their goals.

Precision, safety, and natural results
