01Hormone care

Testosterone replacement for men

Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone, tied to energy, drive, muscle, and mood. After about age 30 it tends to decline gradually — and for some men the drop is faster or steeper, showing up in how they feel day to day.

Low testosterone is a clinical question, not a self-diagnosis. Your clinician reviews your symptoms, history, and labs to determine whether low testosterone explains what you are experiencing.

Signs to watch for

Common symptoms of low testosterone

  • Fatigue
  • Muscle loss
  • Insomnia
  • Sleep apnea
  • Erectile dysfunction
  • Low mood
  • Hot flashes
  • Low sex drive
  • Weight gain
Your options

What can you do about low testosterone?

A simple, clinician-led evaluation — history, examination, and appropriate labs — can determine whether testosterone therapy is an option for you. If it is appropriate, your clinician explains the available options (which may include injectables, pellets, or creams), the monitoring involved, and the risks and alternatives.

Care is matched to you and monitored over time. See hormone replacement therapy or how diagnostic labs are used.

02Hormone care

Sermorelin and growth hormone support

Human growth hormone (GH) supports cell growth and repair, and helps maintain the body's metabolic system in adulthood. GH production naturally declines with age.

Sermorelin is a growth-hormone-releasing hormone that may support the body's own GH production. Whether it is appropriate is a clinical decision based on your goals, health, and labs.

Book a consultation
Signs to watch for

Common symptoms of low growth hormone

  • Muscle loss
  • Fatigue
  • Thinning hair
  • Weight gain
  • Low mood
  • Memory changes
  • Reduced libido
  • Slower recovery
Your options

Is sermorelin right for you?

Sermorelin therapy begins with a clinician evaluation and appropriate lab testing. If it is a fit, GH-related markers are monitored through follow-up labs.

03Hormone care

DHEA replacement for men

DHEA is a hormone produced mainly in the adrenal glands. The body uses it to help make other hormones, including testosterone, and it plays a role in keeping hormones balanced.

Natural DHEA tends to peak in adulthood and decline with age. Your clinician can review whether your levels and symptoms warrant evaluation.

Book a consultation
Signs to watch for

Common symptoms of low DHEA

  • Weight gain
  • Muscle loss
  • Low mood
  • Reduced libido
  • Fatigue
  • Difficulty sleeping
  • Irritability
  • Memory changes
Your options

Treatment for DHEA deficiency

If evaluation shows DHEA may be low and treatment is appropriate, your clinician explains the options (which may include pill or cream forms), dosing, and monitoring — with follow-up to track your response.

04Hormone care

Thyroid hormone support

The thyroid helps regulate metabolism, temperature, weight, and energy. Because thyroid function interacts with your other hormones, low thyroid symptoms often overlap with other imbalances.

Evaluating both thyroid and related hormones helps your clinician see the full picture before recommending anything.

Book a consultation
Signs to watch for

Common symptoms of low thyroid

  • Fatigue
  • Hair loss
  • Weight gain
  • Difficulty sleeping
  • Irritability
  • Low mood
  • Feeling cold
  • Brain fog
Your options

Low thyroid treatment options

Thyroid hormones T3 and T4 help drive metabolism. When evaluation supports it, your clinician may discuss thyroid hormone support with regular lab monitoring to keep levels in an appropriate range.

Questions patients ask

Do I have low testosterone?
That is a clinical question your clinician answers after reviewing your symptoms, history, and appropriate labs. Symptoms alone do not confirm it.

Benefits and risks vary by patient. Your clinician reviews your history, contraindications, alternatives, and monitoring before recommending care.

Duration depends on the reason for treatment, your response, risks, preferences, and medical guidance. A clinician explains the monitoring plan and how treatment may be changed or stopped.
